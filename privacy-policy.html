Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy sets out how What Ya Got? Handyman Services ("we", "us", "our") manages personal information in accordance with the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) contained in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth). We operate in Cairns, Queensland, Australia.

2. Personal Information We Collect

We may collect personal information that you provide to us directly, including:

  • Name and contact details (phone number, mobile, email address).
  • Property address or job location in the Cairns region where handyman work is to be carried out.
  • Photos, videos, or descriptions of repairs you submit for quotes.
  • Billing, tax invoice, and payment information.

3. How We Use Your Information

We collect and use your personal information solely for legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Providing rough quotes, price estimates, and scheduling handyman appointments.
  • Carrying out on-site repair and maintenance services.
  • Communicating with you regarding job status, parts sourcing, and follow-up.
  • Issuing GST tax invoices and managing accounts.

4. Disclosure of Personal Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We may disclose your information only to necessary service providers (such as accounting software or payment processing platforms) solely for the purpose of operating our business, or where required by law.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable precautions to protect personal information from misuse, loss, unauthorized access, modification, or disclosure.
